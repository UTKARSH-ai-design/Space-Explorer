Cosmic Facts

Numbers so staggering they'll redefine your understanding of scale and existence.

109×
Sun to Earth Diameter

The Sun's diameter is about 109 times that of Earth. You could fit over 1.3 million Earths inside the Sun.

🌌
2T+
Galaxies in Observable Universe

Modern estimates suggest there are over 2 trillion galaxies in the observable universe — each containing billions of stars.

💫
299K
Speed of Light (km/s)

Light travels 299,792 km every second. It takes light about 8 minutes 20 seconds to travel from the Sun to Earth.

🌑
-270°C
Temperature of Space

The average temperature of space is -270.45°C (2.7K), just barely above absolute zero — the coldest possible temperature.

🌪️
350yr
Jupiter's Great Red Spot Age

Jupiter's Great Red Spot is a storm that has been continuously raging for over 350 years — larger than Earth itself.

🪐
0.69
Saturn's Density (g/cm³)

Saturn is so low in density it would float on water! It's the only planet in our solar system less dense than water.

The Big Bang & Cosmic Inflation

Approximately 13.8 billion years ago, the universe erupted from an infinitely dense, infinitely hot singularity. In the first fraction of a second, it underwent cosmic inflation — expanding faster than the speed of light — setting the stage for galaxies, stars, planets, and ultimately life itself. Everything you see, touch, or are, traces its origin to that single moment.

⏳ Through Time

Space Exploration Timeline

Humanity's greatest journey — from first satellites to interstellar probes.

1957
Sputnik 1 — First Satellite

The Soviet Union launches Sputnik 1, the first artificial Earth satellite, marking the dawn of the Space Age.

1961
Yuri Gagarin — First Human in Space

Soviet cosmonaut Yuri Gagarin becomes the first human to travel into space aboard Vostok 1, orbiting Earth once.

1969
Apollo 11 — Moon Landing

Neil Armstrong and Buzz Aldrin become the first humans to walk on the Moon. "One giant leap for mankind."

1977
Voyager 1 & 2 — Deep Space

NASA launches the Voyager probes. Voyager 1 is now the farthest human-made object, over 23 billion km from Earth.

1990
Hubble Space Telescope

The Hubble Space Telescope is deployed, forever changing our view of the cosmos with millions of deep-space images.

2004
Mars Rovers — Spirit & Opportunity

NASA's twin Mars Exploration Rovers land on Mars, exploring for years and confirming evidence of ancient liquid water.

2021
James Webb Space Telescope

JWST launches and delivers the deepest infrared images of the universe ever captured, peering back 13.6 billion years.
